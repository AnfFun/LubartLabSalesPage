# Step 05 — Selected work

**Status:** Not started
**Depends on:** Step 04

## Business outcome

The site has a credible structure for future proof without fabricating completed work.

## Scope

- Two or three deliberately designed placeholders.
- Explicit “Кейс готується” state.
- A reusable case-card contract for future screenshots, scope, and result.

## Acceptance criteria

- No fictional client, logo, screenshot, metric, testimonial, or result appears.
- Placeholders feel intentional rather than broken or empty.
- Future real cases can replace placeholders without redesigning the section contract.
- Placeholder motion does not imply unavailable detail is clickable.

## Verification

- Semantic state wording.
- Desktop/mobile layout and focus checks.
