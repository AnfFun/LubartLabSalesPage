## Rejected directions

- **Редакційний маніфест:** too little system language for the integration offer.
- **Центр керування:** too dark and SaaS-like for the intended human sales positioning.
- **Equal light/dark rhythm:** weakened the agreed light-dominant identity.
- **System-first hero:** reduced immediate comprehension of the studio promise.
- **Sticky services:** made the three offer directions slower to scan.
- **Three case slots:** risked implying three specific client projects.
- **Technical-terminal contact:** made the inquiry flow feel colder than intended.
- **Persistent motion in every section:** introduced unnecessary visual and performance cost.

## Intentional implementation boundaries

- Visible supporting copy in the concept is provisional.
- The concept uses no client names, testimonials, metrics, or case-study claims.
- The artboards are design artifacts, not production UI screenshots.
- The temporary motion prototype was removed before the Step 01 commit.
